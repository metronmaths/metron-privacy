Metron Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

No Personal Data Collected

Metron does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no registration, and no analytics or advertising.

Local Storage Only

Your preferences (theme, angle mode, display precision, number format, and startup page) and calculation history are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device.

AI Assistant (Pro+AI tier)

If you subscribe to the Pro+AI plan, your messages are sent to a Metron server (hosted on Google Cloud in the United Kingdom), which checks your subscription is active and then forwards the conversation to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. Only the text of your conversation is transmitted. No personal information is included.

Your conversations are not stored on the server. They are held only on your device, and are passed through in order to answer you. The server keeps just two things: a one-way cryptographic hash of your Google Play subscription token, which identifies your subscription without revealing it, and a running total of how much of your allowance you have used. Neither can be traced back to you personally.

Free and Pro users make no external network requests unless they choose to supply their own API key, in which case their messages go directly to Google and never reach a Metron server.

In-App Purchases

Pro and Pro+AI subscriptions are processed through Google Play. Payment information is handled entirely by Google and is never seen or stored by Metron.

No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising

Metron does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services.

Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal data, Metron is safe to use by people of all ages.

Contact
